Overview

Fight close, stack poison

SAPO is a 3D top-down, bullet hell, boss-fight game where you play as Jaime the frog and defeat the Yakuza Crane by dodging and attacking with your poisonous tongue.

Quickly maneuver and play aggressively to stack poison, while avoiding the boss' attacks.

Combat design

Rewarding aggression

SAPO is a bullet hell built around aggressive play. Players are rewarded for staying close to the boss, supported by mechanics such as poison stacking and boomerang-style projectiles.

This design intentionally subverts the common bullet hell strategy of staying far away from the boss to maximize safety.

Boomerang attack

The player attacks with a boomerang-style projectile that hits enemies on both its outgoing and returning paths, rewarding good positioning around the boss.

Poison stacks

Attacks also apply poison stacks, encouraging players to stay aggressive and maintain constant pressure on the boss.

Boss attack patterns

Controlling space

The boss uses bullet patterns designed to control space and challenge close-range play.

Radial spreads, rotating patterns, and directional barrages force players to dodge while maintaining aggressive positioning.

Game audio

Music & sound

Music composition

Collaborated with the team to define the game's musical identity, contributing to the creative direction and composing original tracks for the Main Menu and Battle sequences, the two core pillars of the game's audio experience.

Sound implementation

Sourced and curated sound effects from the VFS SFX library, editing and refining each asset to fit the game's tone. Integrated all audio into the project using FMOD.
