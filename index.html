About

Hi! I'm Tomas, a Game Designer from Medellín, Colombia, now based in Vancouver, Canada, with a passion for creating experiences that are fun, cohesive and memorable.

Since 2018 I've been building skills across Design, Programming, Production, Art and Audio, because understanding how a game comes together as a whole makes me a better designer for every part of it.

I studied Game Design, UI/UX Design and Digital Entertainment Design Engineering, so I care a lot about how players feel when they play: clear interfaces, good feedback, and experiences that are easy to pick up and hard to put down.

Feel free to reach out! I'd love to talk about games, music, or any project you have in mind.
